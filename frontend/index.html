Undergraduate Research Paper

Semantic–Linguistic Feature Fusion Methodology

Investigating whether combining contextual transformer embeddings with fine-grained linguistic feature vectors improves classification across e-commerce dark patterns.

The Research Hypothesis

Standard transformers encode general distributional semantics but may under-weight explicit, domain-specific syntactic constructions that characterize dark patterns—such as imperative verb moods, explicit countdown units, simulated social proof noun phrases, and comparative pricing modifiers. We hypothesize that late feature fusion provides inductive linguistic bias that enhances precision in ambiguous or subtle instances.

Fusion Architecture Design

Branch A: spaCy-extracted linguistic vector (33 dimensions) → Linear(33, 64) → LayerNorm → GELU → Dropout(0.2).
Branch B: DistilBERT contextual encoder → [CLS] representation (768 dimensions).
Late Fusion Head: Concat(768 + 64 = 832) → Linear(832, 128) → LayerNorm → GELU → Linear(128, 4 classes).
